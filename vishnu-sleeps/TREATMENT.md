# The Sleeping Keeper: Vishnu (treatment)

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 90 s · English voice-over, warm female narrator (Kokoro `af_bella`, speed 0.88), burned-in subtitles + `.srt` · −14 LUFS
**Style:** Watercolor Brush. **Approach:** a fifth Vishnu film, so a subject none of the others touch: his yearly sleep. Devshayani Ekadashi (Ashadha, June–July) begins Chaturmas, the four monsoon months of his yoga-nidra on Shesha; Prabodhini Ekadashi (Kartika, October–November) is his awakening, celebrated in many homes with Tulsi Vivah (the sacred basil married to Vishnu, often under a sugarcane canopy), after which the wedding season begins. Symbols, not portraits: the coiled serpent and a blue haze; the god is never drawn.

## Structures considered
1. The avatars again: done twice. 2. One avatar story: done twice. 3. **The calendar (chosen):** night, then a locked landscape repainted month by month (the style's "change in time on a locked view"), then the courtyard at waking, then a lotus.

## Sound
68 BPM lullaby: harp, cello, bowed vibraphone, flute. One chord per month under rain and thunder; glockenspiel lamps; a rising flute at the awakening; a light frame-drum pattern and Nepalese bells for the wedding; a tubular bell under the title.
