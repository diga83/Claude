Pricing questions

What exactly is an “AI reading”?

You describe your dream in your own words and a state-of-the-art AI composes a personalized interpretation: the dream’s emotional core, its symbols read in your context, a multi-paragraph reading in the lens you choose, and reflection questions. It works on any dream — not just ones containing dictionary symbols.

What happens when I run out of credits?

Nothing breaks. The instant on-device interpreter, the dictionary, your journal, and all guides stay fully available — they’re free forever. Monthly credits refresh on the 1st, and credit packs never expire.

Is my dream content private?

Your journal lives only in your browser and is never uploaded. Instant readings are processed entirely on your device. AI readings send only the dream text you submit, solely to generate your reading — it is not used to build a profile of you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your billing period, you keep any unused pack credits, and nothing you’ve written is ever locked away.
