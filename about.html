About Somnia

Most dream sites pick a side: mystical certainty or scientific dismissal. Somnia’s position is that dreams are genuinely meaningful — just not in the way dream dictionaries claim.

Our methodology

Every symbol in our dictionary and every reading from our interpreter is presented through multiple lenses, clearly labeled:

  • Modern psychology. The best-supported finding in dream research is the continuity hypothesis: dreams reflect waking concerns, relationships, and emotions. Where a symbol has actual research behind it (teeth dreams and dental irritation; exam dreams and performance; prevalence statistics), we cite it.
  • Jungian. Archetypes, the shadow, compensation — an influential interpretive tradition, presented as tradition, not fact.
  • Freudian. Wish fulfillment and latent content — historically foundational, scientifically contested, included for completeness and labeled as such.
  • Cross-cultural. From the Egyptian dream papyri and Artemidorus to Ibn Sirin and the Zhougong Jiemeng, humans have read dreams for four millennia. We note these traditions where they illuminate a symbol — as cultural history, not prophecy.

What we won’t claim

  • That any symbol has one fixed, universal meaning. Research finds it doesn’t.
  • That dreams predict the future. No precognition evidence has survived rigorous scrutiny.
  • That an interpretation can diagnose you. It can’t, and we won’t phrase anything as if it could.

Your privacy

Somnia is a fully static site. The interpreter runs entirely in your browser, and journal entries are stored only in your browser’s local storage. No accounts, no tracking, no dream ever leaves your device. Use the journal’s export button to make your own backups.

Sources

Key research behind our content includes: Nielsen et al. (2003) on typical dream prevalence; Schredl et al. (2016) on lucid dream prevalence; Aspy et al. (2017, 2020) on lucid dream induction; Rozen & Soffer-Dudek (2018) on teeth dreams; the AASM position paper (2018) on nightmare treatment; and Zadra & Stickgold’s NEXTUP model (2021). The full research dossier with claim-by-claim citations and confidence ratings lives in the project’s RESEARCH.md.

Disclaimer

The dream interpretations, articles, and tools on this site are provided for entertainment, self-reflection, and general educational purposes only. Dream meanings are personal and contextual: science does not support universal symbol definitions, and no interpretation here describes a fact about you, your health, or your future. Nothing on this site constitutes medical, psychological, or psychiatric advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare or mental-health professional. Never disregard professional advice, delay seeking it, or change any medication because of something you read here.

If you experience frequent or distressing nightmares, fear of sleeping, nightmares following trauma, sleep paralysis that worries you, acting out your dreams, or thoughts of harming yourself, please contact a doctor or mental-health professional. If you are in crisis in the U.S., call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call your local emergency number.
