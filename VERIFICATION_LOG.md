# Verification Log — *Mary Magdalene and the Memory of Women's Authority*

An auditable record of the two-pass fact-check. Honest verdicts only; **no page numbers were guessed**. Access note: web-level verification; many full-text hosts returned HTTP 403, so interior page-images of modern monographs could not be opened — those page numbers are deliberately left unasserted.

## Pass 1 — references and load-bearing facts

- **33/33 monographs** confirmed real and bibliographically accurate (author, title, publisher, year).
- **6/6 journal articles** confirmed, including page ranges: Schrader & Taylor *JBL* 140(4):751–773; Shoemaker *JECS* 9(4):555–595; Burer & Wallace *NTS* 47(1):76–91; Schrader *HTR* 110(3):360–392 (+ corrigendum 473–474); D'Angelo (105–128); Taylor *PEQ* 146(3):205–223.
- **7/7 primary-source locations** confirmed: Gregory *Homily 33* (not 25); Chrysostom *Hom. Rom.* 31; Pliny 10.96; Tertullian *De virg. vel.* 9; Laodicea canon 11; Chalcedon canon 15 (age 40); Hippolytus.
- **Corrections applied:** *apostola apostolorum* re-dated to the high Middle Ages (the *Vita* ascribed to Rabanus Maurus is now judged pseudonymous/12th-c.); Nag Hammadi tractate count hedged (duplicates; ~45–46 distinct); *ministrae* hedged as "deacons"; reference list fixed (MacDonald lead author of *A Woman's Place*).

## Standing interpretive cautions (keep hedged — do not "resolve")
The *Gospel of Philip* lacuna and its "[mouth]" reconstruction; the etymology of "Magdalene" (Schrader & Taylor's honorific thesis is a contested minority view); the *episcopa*/*presbytera* inscriptions; *ministrae* as a technical office; the validity of "Gnosticism" as a category.
