# Kurma Avatar — The Tortoise Who Held Up the World

**Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) · **Length:** 90 s · **Narration:** soft female voice (Kokoro, blend of *af_heart* + *af_nicole*) · **Style:** "Luminous Myth" — flat 2D shapes in jewel-tone gradients (pearl white, violet, emerald, magenta, gold), gold-line mandalas, soft glows, slow camera pushes and cross-dissolves. Same look as the Matsya and Rama films.

**Score & sound:** original procedural score — tanpura-style drone in D, a slow flute melody in raga Bhupali, harp plucks; a dark drone for the demons, the sinking mountain, the poison and the theft; a frame-drum pulse for the churning. Effects: conch, temple bell, chimes, waves, bubbles, thunder.

## Narration (full text)

Long ago, the gods lost their strength to a sage's curse, and the demons began to win every battle. Desperate, they begged Vishnu for help, and he shared a secret. Churn the ocean of milk, and the nectar of immortality will rise. The task was so huge that gods and demons had to work together. They used Mount Mandara as a churning rod, and the serpent Vasuki as the rope. with the gods at its tail, and the demons at its head. But as they pulled, the mountain began to sink. So Vishnu became Kurma, a giant tortoise, and dove beneath the waves to carry the mountain on his back. Steady at last, the mountain spun, and the ocean foamed. First came a deadly poison, dark enough to end the world, but Shiva drank it, and his throat turned blue forever. Then the treasures rose. A wish granting cow, a white elephant, a tree of wishes, the shining moon, and Lakshmi, goddess of fortune, seated on a lotus. At last, the healer Dhanvantari appeared, holding the pot of nectar. The demons snatched it, but Vishnu, disguised as the enchanting Mohini, won it back for the gods. Their strength returned, and the heavens were safe again. And through it all, Kurma never moved. This is Kurma, the second avatar of Vishnu, a reminder that the greatest strength is sometimes just holding steady.
