## Notes
- **Colour key** (consistent across figures): deep red = erasure; green = heterodox contestation; blue = reclamation / canonical witness; gold = secondary/ambivalent.
- The data figures (3, 6, 7) encode scholarly *contestation* explicitly (half-markers, daggers) rather than presenting disputed points as settled — consistent with the article's method.
- Source script: `generate_figures.py` (matplotlib). Re-run to regenerate or restyle.
