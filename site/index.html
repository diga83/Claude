Welcome to Al Injaz

A Trusted International School in the Heart of Muscat

For over a decade, Al Injaz International Private School has been recognised as one of the best schools in Muscat for families seeking a rigorous, values-driven and globally connected education. Set on a purpose-built campus in Al Ansab, we combine the world-renowned Cambridge curriculum with a strong bilingual programme rooted in the culture and language of Oman.

From our nurturing Kindergarten to our ambitious Sixth Form, every learner is known, challenged and supported. Our inclusive ethos — including a dedicated Special Educational Needs department — ensures that every child has the opportunity to thrive.
