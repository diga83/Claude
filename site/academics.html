Academics & Curriculum

A seamless, internationally accredited learning journey from Kindergarten to Grade 12 — combining the Cambridge curriculum with a strong bilingual foundation.

Our Curriculum

Cambridge Excellence, Bilingual Strength

At Al Injaz, students follow the globally respected Cambridge International curriculum, progressing through Cambridge Primary, Lower Secondary, IGCSE and AS & A Levels. These qualifications are recognised by leading universities around the world and provide a clear, rigorous pathway to higher education.

Alongside Cambridge, our bilingual programme — aligned with the Sultanate of Oman's Ministry of Education — ensures students develop fluency in both Arabic and English while staying connected to their culture and identity. The result is a balanced education that opens global doors without losing local roots.

Learning Stages

Four Stages, One Vision

Kindergarten

Ages 3–5

Our Kindergarten provides a safe, joyful and stimulating start to school. Through play-based learning, children develop early literacy and numeracy, language skills in Arabic and English, and the social and emotional foundations for lifelong learning.

Primary

Grades 1–6

In Primary, students follow the Cambridge Primary programme alongside bilingual studies. We build strong foundations in English, Mathematics, Science and Arabic, while developing curiosity, independence and a genuine love of learning through engaging, well-resourced lessons.

Secondary

Grades 7–10 · IGCSE

Secondary students progress through Cambridge Lower Secondary into the IGCSE programme. With a broad choice of subjects, expert teaching and regular assessment, learners are challenged to think critically, work independently and prepare for advanced study and examinations.

Sixth Form

Grades 11–12 · A Level

Our Sixth Form offers Cambridge AS & A Level pathways with dedicated university and careers guidance. Students specialise in their chosen subjects, develop the academic skills universities value, and receive personal support to secure places at top institutions in Oman and abroad.
