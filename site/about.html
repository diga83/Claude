About Our School

A community committed to academic excellence, character and care — proudly serving families across Muscat and the Sultanate of Oman.

Who We Are

A Purpose-Built School for the Whole Child

Al Injaz International Private School is a coeducational, KG–Grade 12 institution located on a purpose-built campus in Al Ansab, Muscat. We bring together the academic rigour of the Cambridge International curriculum and a bilingual programme rooted in the language, values and heritage of Oman.

Our school is built on a simple belief: that every child deserves an education that develops not only their academic ability, but also their character, creativity and confidence. With modern facilities, dedicated teachers and a genuinely inclusive culture, we have become a trusted choice for families seeking the best school in Muscat.

Our Mission

To provide an outstanding, inclusive education that empowers every student to achieve academic excellence, embrace strong values and become a responsible global citizen.

Our Vision

To be recognised as one of the leading international schools in Oman — a place where tradition and innovation meet to prepare confident learners for a changing world.

Our Values

Respect, integrity, perseverance and compassion guide everything we do — shaping a caring community where students feel safe, valued and inspired.

A Message from the Principal

"At Al Injaz, we know each child by name and by need. Our purpose is to unlock the potential within every learner — academically, socially and personally — so they leave us ready to lead meaningful lives. We warmly invite your family to become part of our story."

The Principal

Special Needs & Inclusion

An Inclusive School Where Every Child Belongs

Inclusion is at the very heart of who we are. Al Injaz is proud to operate a dedicated Special Educational Needs (SEN) department — a feature that sets us apart among schools in Muscat.

Our specialist educators work closely with families to create individual learning plans, provide in-class and one-to-one support, and ensure that children of all abilities learn side by side. We believe diversity strengthens our community and that every learner has a unique contribution to make.
