Admissions

Joining Al Injaz is simple. We welcome applications throughout the year for Kindergarten to Grade 12, subject to availability.

How to Apply

Four Simple Steps to Enrolment

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Complete the enquiry form below or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us your child's age and the grade you're interested in.

  2. 2

    Visit & Tour

    Our admissions team will arrange a campus tour and answer all your questions about school life.

  3. 3

    Assessment

    A friendly age-appropriate assessment helps us place your child in the right class and provide the right support.

  4. 4

    Enrol

    Submit the required documents, confirm your place, and welcome to the Al Injaz family!

Required Documents

What You'll Need

  • Completed application / enquiry form
  • Copy of the student's passport & resident card (if applicable)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school reports / transfer certificate
  • Birth certificate & vaccination record

Tuition & Fees

Fee structures vary by grade level. Please contact our admissions office for the current fee schedule and any available scholarships or sibling discounts.
