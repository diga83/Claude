# The Way Home: Rama (treatment)

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 90 s · English voice-over, warm female narrator (Kokoro `af_bella`, speed 0.88), burned-in subtitles + `.srt` · −14 LUFS
**Style:** Watercolor Brush. **Approach:** the Ramayana told as one journey on one painted column, north at the top and south at the bottom, so that the story's geography is the camera move. Symbols and small figures, not portraits: footprints, a golden deer, a dark streak across the sky, a trail of jewels, a monkey army, a gold arc for Hanuman's leap, the bridge of floating stones, an arrow, a lotus, lamps. Content follows the common telling (Valmiki and popular tradition): fourteen years of exile, Maricha as the golden deer, Sita's dropped ornaments, Hanuman's leap, the Rama Setu with Rama's name on the stones (a popular tradition), Ravana's fall, the lamps of the homecoming linked with Diwali in many regions.

## Structures considered
1. Seven tableaux of the epic: too many cuts for 90 s.
2. One character's view (Sita, Hanuman): strong, but it narrows "about Rama".
3. **A single road from Ayodhya to Lanka and back (chosen):** the camera tilts down the road as the story goes south, then climbs all the way back up as the lamps light, a reversal that is also the ending.

## Sound
72 BPM; harp, cello, flute, with one harmony per place (D → Bm → Em → G → A → Bm → D). Footsteps, forest, hooves for the deer, a rush for the abduction, falling jewel tinkles, the sea, monkey chatter, a splash per stone; frame drum and gong for the battle; lamp crackles and bells for the return; tubular bell under the title. Effects were moved off the words they masked ("leapt", "to find her").
