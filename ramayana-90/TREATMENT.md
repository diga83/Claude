# The Ramayana in 90 Seconds (treatment)

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 90 s · English voice-over, warm female narrator (Kokoro `af_bella`, speed 0.92), burned-in subtitles + `.srt` · −14 LUFS
**Style:** Watercolor Brush.
**The brief:** the story in detail, with no empty spaces and no silences. So:
- **Narration is wall to wall:** 14 lines from 0.5 s to 84.2 s, with at most 0.3 s between them. Music and the closing title carry the last 6 s.
- **There are no empty frames:** each of the 13 panels is full-bleed, its background already painted when a brush-edge wipe reveals it. No frame is more than about a fifth plain paper.
- **The sound never drops out:** a continuous harp line with one chord per chapter, a flute between the lines, and an ambience bed under every panel that overlaps the next. The quietest quarter-second is about −33 dB.

## The thirteen chapters (one panel each)
| # | Chapter | What is painted, and the one action |
|---|---|---|
| I | Ayodhya: four princes | city walls in gold; four lotuses open, the last larger (Rama) |
| II | Mithila: Shiva's bow | a great strung bow; a marigold garland; the bow **snaps** on "snapped in two" |
| III | Two promises: Kaikeyi's claim | a crown on a cushion between two lamps; two sealed promises appear |
| IV | Exile: fourteen years | three walkers (Rama with a bow, Sita, Lakshmana) recede down a road into the forest |
| V | The sandals: Bharata's throne | a golden throne; Rama's sandals placed on it; a lamp |
| VI | Panchavati: the golden deer | forest; the deer leaps across; Rama follows with his bow |
| VII | The abduction: Jatayu falls | a dark chariot streak; Sita's jewels fall; the vulture beats its wings, then falls |
| VIII | Kishkindha: Sugriva and Hanuman | rocky hills; the monkey army gathers; Hanuman appears, with a mace and a gold glow |
| IX | Lanka: the ring, the fire | the leap arc; the ring glows in the red Ashoka grove; Lanka's towers catch fire |
| X | Rama Setu: the floating stones | 22 stones marked in vermilion laid across the sea to the island |
| XI | The war: Ravana's fall | a rain of arrows; one gold arrow; Ravana's dark crown topples |
| XII | Homecoming: the lamps | Ayodhya at night; 36 lamps light row by row; a crown descends (the coronation) |
| XIII | Diwali: lit again | the lamps, and a rangoli painted around one lamp; title RAMA, *the one who kept his word* |

## Sound
72 BPM, one chord per chapter (D, G, Bm, Em, A, G, Bm, D, Em, A, Bm, G, D) on harp, bowed vibraphone and cello, with a quiet flute. There's a gong as the bow snaps and a frame-drum pulse through the war. Foley: footsteps, hooves, a rush, jewels, wings, monkey chatter, the ring's chime, fire, a splash per stone, arrows, lamps and bells. The pen and whoosh sounds were turned down or moved where they masked words.
