Biography

Educator, Author & Collaborator

Subhashini Sumanasekara is a distinguished ICT educator with over two decades of experience, dedicated to guiding students from diverse backgrounds. She holds a Master of Science in Strategic IT Management from the University of Wolverhampton, which sharpened her expertise in technology and its role in education.

She has further strengthened her educational leadership through a postgraduate diploma in Education Management and Leadership from OTHM, reflecting a deep commitment to excellence in teaching and school leadership. Keeping pace with the rapidly evolving field of ICT, she takes a keen interest in contemporary developments such as artificial intelligence and its responsible use in the classroom.

Beyond her work in education, Subhashini is passionate about the moral and emotional development of children through literature. She believes strongly in the power of children's books to shape young minds, encourage empathy, and nurture values — a conviction that shines through in every book we write together.

Our Writing Partnership

Subhashini is the co-author of the Bible Adventure Series — the children's Bible picture-book collection at the heart of our catalogue — along with many of our other children's titles, including Sasha's Adventures, which we created together and named after our daughter.

Our collaboration brings together complementary strengths: her background in education and child development, and my work in writing and digital publishing. The result is a large, cohesive library of children's books designed to teach, delight, and inspire young readers around the world.

We share our life and our work in Muscat, Oman, alongside our daughter Sasha — forming a family rooted in educational values, creativity, and a love of learning.
