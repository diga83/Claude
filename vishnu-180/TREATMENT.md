# An Ocean, a Step, a Stone: Vishnu (treatment)

**Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16, like the rest of the series), 30 fps, 180 s · English voice-over, warm female narrator (Kokoro `af_bella`, speed 0.88), burned-in subtitles + `.srt` · −14 LUFS
**Style:** Watercolor Brush. **Approach:** symbols, not portraits. Vishnu is a blue haze, a footprint, a stone. Content follows widely shared tradition: Ananta Shesha on the ocean of milk; Lakshmi; the common reading of his name as "the all-pervading"; conch, discus, mace, lotus; Garuda; the Vamana / Trivikrama story; Padmanabhaswamy at Thiruvananthapuram ("city of Ananta"), seen through three doors; Bhagavad Gita 4.7–8; the shaligram (ammonite fossils from the Kali Gandaki, about 150 million years old).

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **The ten avatars as a list.** Already done in *The Three Answers*; rejected.
2. **The four objects in his hands as plates.** Already the Brahma film's grammar; rejected.
3. **A line without an end (chosen).** It opens with one stroke that keeps painting, the endless serpent, and ends with an ammonite's spiral carried on outward as the same line, still painting when the film stops. In between: the ocean turns blue, Garuda, the three steps (the signature scale shot), the three doors, the promise, and the river stone.

## 2. Logline and arc
*One stroke that never ends: the serpent he rests on, the steps that measure the worlds, the stone that holds him in a river.*
Setup: ocean, Ananta, Vishnu and Lakshmi, the colour of sky and ocean, his emblems. Development: Garuda; Vamana grows and takes two steps, earth and heavens; the king bows for the third. Turn: the temple that can only show him in three parts; the promise to return. Ending: the shaligram, and the spiral that becomes the endless line.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Kerala murals; Pahari miniature skies** (grammar only): blue as the divine colour, temple roofs, gold stars. *Don't take:* any figure, composition or real temple drawing.
- **Specimen plates** (the fossil): one object, a measured age, a place.

## 5. Cue map / sound
70 BPM, G major. Harp and bowed vibraphone throughout; cello drone; flute melodies in the open moments. A horn for the conch, a glockenspiel for Lakshmi's coins, wingbeats and a cry for Garuda. A string swell as Vamana grows, and a **bass drum + gong for each giant step**, with held breaths before the first step and before the bow. Hand chimes for the three doors, a river bed, a tubular bell under the title. Foley is ducked deeper than the music under speech; brush sounds that masked names were moved.
