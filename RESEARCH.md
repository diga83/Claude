# Research Dossier — Somnia Dream Interpretation Site

Compiled June 2026 via parallel web research across five angles, with claim-by-claim
source attribution and confidence ratings. **Method note:** direct page fetching was
blocked in the research environment (HTTP 403 on all domains), so claims were drawn from
search-engine content extracts and cross-checked across multiple independent results.
Claims rated High appeared consistently across several sources and/or trace to
peer-reviewed or primary references. Starred items should be verified against the
primary source before being used in high-stakes contexts.

## 5. Competitive feature research (what leading dream sites/apps offer)

- **Dream dictionaries** are the traffic engine of every incumbent (DreamMoods ~5,800 entries + 23 theme categories; AuntyFlo 5,000+; DreamBible A–Z). Long-tail "dream meaning" SEO is the core model.
- **AI interpretation with selectable lenses** (Jungian/Freudian) is the modern differentiator (DreamApp, Elsewhere, SomniAI, Oniri) — typically freemium (e.g., 3 free analyses/month).
- **Journals** with tags, moods, voice notes, recurring-symbol detection are standard in apps (Oniri, Elsewhere, Dreamboard, Lucidity, Dream Journal Ultimate); stats/insights dashboards and streaks are premium staples.
- **Lucid-dreaming tooling** (technique guides, reality-check reminders, WBTB alarms) appears in Lucidity, Oniri, Capture; r/LucidDreaming and DreamViews show community demand.
- **Privacy** is an active selling point (SomniAI markets E2E encryption; Lucidity offers local storage).
- Recommended must-haves implemented in Somnia: dictionary + categories + instant search, multi-lens interpreter, private journal with stats, dark-mode-first mobile design, lucid/recall/nightmare guides, prominent disclaimers. (Accounts, AI-art generation, and community features were noted as server-dependent and out of scope for a static site.)

Confidence caveat: competitor specifics (entry counts, prices) came from search summaries
only and should be spot-checked before use in marketing copy.
