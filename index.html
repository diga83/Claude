Did you know?

You dream ~2 hours a night

Across 4–6 REM periods that lengthen toward morning — the last dream of the night can approach an hour.

55% have had a lucid dream

A meta-analysis of ~24,000 people found over half experience lucidity at least once, and 23% monthly. It’s trainable.

Symbols aren’t universal

Research finds no fixed meanings — the same image can mean opposite things to different dreamers. Your associations matter most.
