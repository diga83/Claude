🎯 Command Word Drills

The same scenario, asked with different command words. See how your answer must change for each one. This is where most marks are won or lost in GCSE Computer Science.

📊 What the real papers actually ask (analysis of the 2019–2025 Paper 1s)

The most frequent command words are Give, State, Complete, Identify, Describe and Explain — quick 1–2 mark parts make up most of the paper. Construct appears for flowcharts, truth tables and logic statements, Convert for binary/hex, and there is exactly one 6-mark Discuss per paper. Master the short answers first: they carry the majority of the 80 marks.
